**Situation:** You have reached a key inflection point where disparate threads of your life—technical skills (PLM, ERP, CRM), intellectual pursuits (economic theory), and community involvement (church)—are converging around a powerful new capability: AI-driven simulation.

**Complication:** Previously, your ability to apply your enterprise systems knowledge was blocked by high technical barriers (e.g., complex software installation) and the lack of a compelling, data-rich "business case." This created a gap between your theoretical knowledge and practical, portfolio-worthy application.

**Key Question:** How can you leverage AI-driven simulation to bridge the gap between your technical expertise and a tangible, high-impact project, thereby building your consulting skills and creating a powerful professional portfolio?

**Answer:** The immediate opportunity is to architect and build a **simulated, end-to-end operational model** for your church's "Tiny Homes for Aged-Out Orphans" initiative. This project serves as the perfect "business case," allowing you to deploy your skills in a low-risk, high-impact environment. You will use AI to overcome the traditional barriers of data generation (CAD files, BOMs) and technical setup, creating a "digital twin" of the nonprofit venture.

This approach directly addresses your core insight: **"Code is cheap. It's all about the business case."** You have found the ideal case.

## Recommended Next Steps: An 8-Week Action Plan

This plan is designed to move from concept to a tangible, presentable asset in eight weeks.

### Phase 1: Scoping & Validation (Weeks 1-2)

1.  **Formalize the Engagement:** Follow up with Pastor Andy. Frame your offer not as "technical help," but as a strategic project to create a **planning and fundraising model** for the tiny homes initiative. Define the immediate pain points (QuickBooks, CRM) and identify the key stakeholders.
2.  **Validate the Core Technology:** Spend one afternoon using AI tools to **generate a sample CAD model** of a retrofitted shipping container tiny home. This is a quick win that validates the foundational concept of AI-generated substance.
3.  **Draft a Project Charter:** Create a one-page document outlining the project's vision, key deliverables (a slide deck demonstrating the simulated operation), and a timeline. This brings professional structure to the volunteer effort.

---

### Phase 2: System & Substance Buildout (Weeks 3-6)

4.  **Deploy Sandbox Environments:** Using AI assistance for installation guides, set up free-tier or open-source instances of the three core systems:
    * **PLM** (Product Lifecycle Management)
    * **ERP** (Enterprise Resource Planning)
    * **CRM** (Customer Relationship Management)
5.  **Generate Simulated Data:**
    * **PLM:** Generate 3-5 distinct tiny home designs (CAD files).
    * **ERP:** Extract Bill of Materials (BOM) from the CAD files and simulate costs, suppliers, and a project budget.
    * **CRM:** Based on the church's needs, create and populate a synthetic database of volunteers, donors, and community partners.
6.  **Populate the Systems:** Ingest the simulated data into your sandbox environments. The goal is to create a living, interconnected model.

---

### Phase 3: Demonstration of Value (Weeks 7-8)

7.  **Develop Key Insights:** Create dashboards and reports within the simulated systems that answer critical questions for the church. For example:
    * *“What is the projected material cost for a 10-unit build?”* (ERP)
    * *“Which of our volunteers have construction skills?”* (CRM)
    * *“What are the design variations we can offer donors?”* (PLM)
8.  **Build the Presentation:** Consolidate all screenshots, dashboards, and insights into a crisp, professional slide deck. This document is your **primary portfolio asset**.
9.  **Present to Stakeholders:** Schedule a formal presentation with Pastor Andy and the church leadership. Walk them through the model, demonstrating how this forward-thinking approach can enhance planning, secure funding, and streamline execution for their mission.
