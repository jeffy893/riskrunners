## The Big Trust: What Was — Is For What Will Be

`UA-RiskRunners-Presentation/`

Presented to the Risk Runners Club at the University of Arizona on February 17, 2026. Available in Keynote, PowerPoint, PDF, and Marp Markdown formats.

### Talk Summary

Jefferson Richards, founder of the Risk Runners Club at U of A, shares his journey from founding the club through actuarial science, NLP, insurance internships, and into captive insurance consulting.

### Key Themes

**Origins: LinguaLint and NLP**
- Built LinguaLint to scrape 1,500 news articles/day and extract event codes for stock market prediction
- Described as a "lightweight LLM middleware" — 10 fingers coding vs. the 100,000 that built ChatGPT
- Pivoted from UC Davis (Agriculture) → Poetry → U of A (Math/Finance)

**Founding Risk Runners**
- Chose U of A over ASU despite ASU having the established actuarial program
- Founded the club to bridge the gap — driving students to WSIA events, building community from scratch

**The Actuarial Internship at Nautilus Insurance**
- Applied NLP to scrape Yelp reviews for hotel/motels
- Discovered risk gaps: "Great mimosa!" + no alcohol indemnification = uncovered exposure
- Win-win-win: underwriter knows risk early, insurer gets proper premiums, client gets real coverage

**Enterprise Architecture of Insurance**
- Floor 1: Claims and Database Admins (foundation)
- Floor 2: Underwriters and Actuaries (risk assessment)
- Floor 3: Financial Analysts and CEO (investment/strategy)
- Key insight: insurance makes money by investing the float, not just on premiums

**A Tale of Two Societies**
| CAS (Casualty) | SOA (Society of Actuaries) |
|----------------|---------------------------|
| Property/Product focus | Health/Life focus |
| Before/After lifecycle risk | During lifecycle risk |
| "The Lawyer" | "The Doctor" |
| ASU (State School) | U of A (University) |

**Career Trajectory**
- CPG Analytics → Tableau mastery → teaching others to build portfolios with Kaggle + AI
- VA Contractor → Patient Generated Health Data (wearables predicting health risks)
- Current: Captive insurance for prefab manufacturers at [captive.integralmass.com](https://captive.integralmass.com)
