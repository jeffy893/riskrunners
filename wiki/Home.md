# RiskRunners Wiki

Welcome to the RiskRunners wiki — the knowledge base for [github.com/jeffy893/riskrunners](https://github.com/jeffy893/riskrunners).

RiskRunners is a collection of actuarial science tools, AI-powered risk analysis platforms, antifragile simulations, and research projects. The repo is maintained by [@jeffy893](https://github.com/jeffy893) (Jefferson Richards) under the MIT License.
