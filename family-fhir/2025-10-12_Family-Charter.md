### Phase 1: The Audit

First, we simulate the data collection process by consolidating the client's insurance portfolio into a single, structured dataset. This captures all relevant policies, the assets they cover, and most importantly, the specific exclusions that represent behavioral risks.

### Phase 2: The Risk Matrix Analysis

Next, the raw data is processed into a **Risk Matrix**. This model identifies and quantifies the "Value at Risk" associated with specific behaviors that could void insurance coverage. The matrix is sorted to prioritize the most significant financial exposures, allowing the consultation to focus on what matters most.

Here is the generated Risk Matrix for our client:

| Asset Covered | Value at Risk ($) | Associated Risk Behavior | Key Exclusion | Policy Type |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

This visualization clearly shows the financial scale of these behavioral risks, making the abstract concept of "risk" tangible for the client.

### Phase 3: The Family Charter Workshop

Finally, the insights from the Risk Matrix are translated into a simple, clear **Family Charter** or **Behavioral Bylaws**. This document serves as the primary deliverable for the client, converting complex insurance jargon into actionable family rules designed to protect their legacy.

## Draft: The 'Jim' Family Behavioral Bylaws
