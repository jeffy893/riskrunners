I will focus on only one relation to generate a function
Bullard Existence Relation: {loss,risk,tariff}

To complete the ordered set, we determine the antonyms of each concept:
Bullard Existence Relation: {loss,risk,tariff} - {gain, impossibility, rebate}
p(gain) = 1 - p(loss)
p(impossibility) = 1 - p(risk)
p(rebate) = 1 - p(tariff)

When we do not know the probability of any of the  [this is a function -->]    Existence(Bullard), we assume the probability to be linearly distributed:
{loss,risk,tariff} - {rebate, impossibility, gain}
{1/6,2/6,3/6} - {...}
{0.167, 0.333, 0.5} - {0.5, 0.667, 0.8333}

(Would have worked better with more than 3 concepts because rebate and tariff are both 0.5)

Which would be a linear distribution when the sets are combined
--> {loss,risk,tariff/rebate,impossibility,gain}
--> {0.167, 0.333, 0.5, 0.667, 0.8333}

Interpretation without knowing true probabilities: "There is a 50/50% chance that it is impossible for there to be a gain with a tariff/rebate." (Like, come on Bullard - you fucking fence rider)

Let's say we know the probability of the existence of a tariff is 0.65, then the probabilities become:
{loss,risk,tariff} - {rebate, impossibility, gain}
{(1-0.65)x(1/3) , (1-0.65)x(2/3) , 0.65} - {...}
{0.11667, 0.23333, 0.65} - {0.35, 0.76667, 0.88333}

Which allows us to combine and order the entire set:
{loss,risk,rebate,tariff,impossibility,gain}
{0.11667,0.23333, 0.35,0.65,0.76667, 0.88333}
